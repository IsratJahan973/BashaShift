## Five-slide pitch outline

1. **Problem:** Finding rentals and arranging a move are disconnected, fragmented tasks. Workers often coordinate by phone.
2. **Solution:** BashaShift, a rental-to-relocation platform with owner listings and independent local mover quotes.
3. **How it works:** Search → ask owner → start move → inventory/estimate → local offer → call/confirm.
4. **Meaningful AI + technology:** Vision scan helps draft inventory (human-corrected); deterministic pricing logic; React/Supabase/Node (name only what is actually deployed).
5. **Solo maker + impact/limits/roadmap:** Name, school/university (only if comfortable), genuine tested capabilities, how to verify owners/workers and expand.

## Suggested English demo script (~75–90 seconds)

"Finding a rental home is only half the problem. After choosing a place, many Bangladeshi families must separately find transport and moving workers. BashaShift brings these steps into one experience."

"Here, I search for a rental in Banasree, compare its rent, and request a viewing. Once I've chosen the destination, I start a moving request. I create an inventory — and when our live AI image scan is configured, a photo can suggest visible furniture items for me to correct."

"Now switch to a local mover's view. The worker sees a simple nearby job card and offers a price. Back as the customer, I choose the worker and call them to confirm. The website records that choice, but does not pretend a click alone guarantees the moving service."
